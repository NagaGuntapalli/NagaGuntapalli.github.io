About

From experimental flames to decision-ready models.

I am a Mechanical Engineering Ph.D. researcher at Louisiana State University studying lean blowout, emissions, fuel-property effects, and sustainable aviation fuels in swirl-stabilized gas-turbine combustors.

My work turns experimental datasets into interpretable analytical, machine-learning, and reduced-order models. I bring that same systems approach to aerospace simulation, safety-algorithm calibration, and quantitative research for energy markets.

I am interested in industry and research roles where physics, data, and engineering judgment come together to improve energy and mobility systems.
