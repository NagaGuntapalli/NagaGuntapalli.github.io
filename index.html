About

Welcome!

I am a Mechanical Ph.D. Candidate at Louisiana State University currently working on fuel property influence on lean blowout limits for a gas turbine combustor, Emissions, Gas Chromatography techniques to find fuel compositions for sustainable aviation fuels. I use experiments to generate the sufficient data sets and then utilize analytical and physics based models to understand the underlying physics and to predict the behavior of the system.

I graduate with a Masters in Aerospace Engineering, specialized in Aerodynamics and Flight Mechanics, I enjoyed my time programming about Space Flight Dynamics, Flight Mechanics, Mission Design, and Multidisciplinary Optimization along with Scientific Machine Learning, After graduation I worked as a Calibration Engineer at Veoneer Safety Systems, applied the MDO principles to optimize safety algorithms specific to a vehicle.

My interests lie in applying my knowledge towards Quantitative Roles, where data driven physics based models are utilized. Currently I'm working on building a Quantitative Investment Model which could be used for predictive analytics with the available market data.

Energy · Combustion

Fuel-Property Influence on Lean-Blowout Characteristics of Liquid Hydrocarbon Fuels

This study combines lean-blowout experiments, analytical timescale calculations, and numerical simulations to examine how fuel properties affect spray breakup, evaporation, chemical processes, and combustor stability across Jet-A and sustainable aviation fuel blends.

Energy · Emissions

Fuel-Property and Combustor-Condition Influence on Emissions Generated by Sustainable Aviation Fuels

This ongoing study examines how fuel composition and combustor operating conditions affect NOx and soot emissions from Jet-A and a sustainable aviation fuel blend. Measurements from a laboratory swirl combustor will be combined with scaling analysis and turbine-engine data to connect fuel properties with engine-relevant emissions.

  • SAF blends
  • NOx
  • Soot & LII
  • Emissions scaling
Work in progress

Energy · Fuel Composition Analysis

Real-Time Composition Sensing, Property Prediction, and Blend Optimization of Sustainable Aviation Fuels

This work develops a closed-loop SAF blending framework that combines real-time composition sensing with hybrid GPR–UNIFAC property prediction. The system will optimize fuel blends to achieve target properties within defined uncertainty limits.

  • GC×GC-VUV
  • GPR–UNIFAC
  • Property prediction
  • Closed-loop blending
